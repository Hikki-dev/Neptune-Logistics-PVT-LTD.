04 / What We Do

Six services. One accountable team.

01
Same-Day Customs Clearance
Government-licensed CHA clearing cargo within 24 hours at all Sri Lankan ports and airports. Full HS-code classification and regulatory document management, stationed inside Colombo Port and Bandaranaike International Airport.
02
Sea / Ocean Freight
FCL and LCL routes worldwide, with direct space allocations from major carriers, weekly LCL consolidation to key regional hubs, and cost-optimized combination sea-air routing.
03
Air Freight
Sri Lanka's leading perishable air cargo agent, seafood, fresh produce and flowers, with continuous cold-chain monitoring and access to freighter charters for urgent or out-of-gauge shipments.
04
Warehousing & Distribution
Secure, inventory-managed storage in Colombo with 24/7 CCTV, access control and fire suppression. Digitized stock tracking, packing, labeling, and rapid cross-docking.
05
Port Handling
In-house operations at Colombo, Hambantota and Trincomalee ports, vessel loading/unloading oversight, container tallying, quay transfer, and direct customs-gate coordination.
06
Last Mile Delivery
An asset-owned fleet of prime movers, container trailers and delivery vans providing island-wide dispatch and doorstep fulfillment for commercial cargo.
