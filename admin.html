Milestone Timeline Strategy:

Neptune's timeline system is automatically computed. Staff simply check boxes for processes that have completed (e.g. customs clearance, cargo picked up). The tracking script automatically establishes the next unchecked step as the active milestone on the public tracking portal.

Role Authorization Rules:

• Operations Staff can query records and update shipment statuses, check milestone checkboxes, and append public notes.
• Administrators have full database privileges, including creating new shipments, deleting records, and generating staff credentials.
