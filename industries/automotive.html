Frequently Asked Questions

How does Neptune Logistics support automotive manufacturers in Sri Lanka?
Neptune Logistics provides just-in-time parts delivery, same-day customs clearance at Colombo Port, and heavy machinery transport for automotive manufacturers in Sri Lanka. All services are handled in-house with zero outsourcing.
Can Neptune handle the import of heavy manufacturing equipment?
Yes. Neptune manages the full import process for heavy manufacturing machinery — sea freight booking, port handling, customs clearance, and inland transport to your factory anywhere in Sri Lanka.
What is just-in-time logistics and does Neptune support it?
Just-in-time (JIT) logistics delivers parts exactly when needed on the production line, minimising inventory costs and downtime. Neptune coordinates JIT delivery schedules with same-day customs clearance and direct port gate release.
