Frequently Asked Questions

Does Neptune Logistics handle cold chain pharmaceutical shipments?
Yes. Neptune provides temperature-controlled air freight and refrigerated sea container shipping for vaccines, biologics, and medical devices. We maintain cold chain integrity from origin to final delivery in Sri Lanka.
Can Neptune clear pharmaceutical imports at Colombo Port and BIA?
Yes. As a licensed Customs House Agent, Neptune provides priority same-day customs clearance for pharmaceutical and healthcare cargo at both Colombo Port and BIA, coordinating with NMRA and other regulatory authorities where required.
What temperature ranges can Neptune maintain for medical cargo?
Neptune coordinates shipments across standard pharmaceutical temperature ranges including 2°C to 8°C (refrigerated), 15°C to 25°C (controlled room temperature), and deep-frozen where applicable, using validated cold chain equipment and continuous temperature logging.
