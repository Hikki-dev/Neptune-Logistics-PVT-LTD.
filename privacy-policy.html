Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 20, 2026

At Neptune Logistics (Pvt) Ltd, we prioritize the protection and confidentiality of your personal and business data. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and secure information when you visit our website or interact with our cargo, freight forwarding, and customs clearance services.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is necessary to provide you with accurate shipping quotes and logistics services. This includes:

  • Contact Details: Your name, business email address, and telephone number.
  • Company Details: Company name and organization size.
  • Inquiry Details: Requested freight service type (Sea Freight, Air Freight, Customs, etc.), cargo origin/destination, and any custom message details you submit via our quote form.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to facilitate safe and efficient shipping operations, specifically:

  • To compile and send you accurate logistics and freight forwarding quotes.
  • To coordinate and execute cargo bookings, customs clearance, and road transport.
  • To answer inquiries and provide support regarding your active cargo status.
  • To comply with international shipping regulations, port guidelines, and Sri Lanka Customs requirements.

3. Data Protection and Zero Outsourcing

Because Neptune operates as a 100% in house freight logistics company, we handle your data with the same direct oversight that we apply to your cargo. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal or business information to third party marketing companies. Data is only shared with authorized customs, port authorities, and transport offices as required to execute your shipment.

4. Cookies and Web Analytics

Our website utilizes minor cookies to optimize user experience and analyze website traffic. You can choose to disable cookies through your personal browser settings, although this may affect functionality when using certain interactive elements of the website (such as our interactive 3D globe or country pills).

5. Your Rights and Compliance

Depending on your location, you may have rights under the GDPR or other local data privacy regulations. These rights include accessing the personal data we hold about you, requesting corrections, or requesting the complete deletion of your records. To make a request, contact our data coordinator at info@neptunelogistics.lk.

6. Policy Updates

We reserve the right to modify this policy periodically to align with changing maritime, air freight, and digital data protection laws. Any updates will be published directly to this page with the modified "Last Updated" date.
