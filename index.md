# Neptune Logistics (Pvt) Ltd

**Freight Forwarding & Customs Clearance — Sri Lanka**

Neptune Logistics is Sri Lanka's trusted in-house logistics provider offering end-to-end supply chain solutions across 60+ countries — no middlemen, every step handled by Neptune.

---

## Services

### Sea / Ocean Freight
Our core offering: FCL and LCL ocean freight forwarding for international cargo. Direct carrier relationships, competitive rates, and in-house port operations.

### Air Freight
A leading name in perishable air cargo with special handling facilities. We charter dedicated freighters and manage temperature-sensitive shipments door-to-door.

### Same Day Customs Clearance
Government licensed Customs House Agent clearing your cargo within 24 hours. As soon as your goods arrive at the port, our dedicated team clears them same day with complete regulatory compliance.

### Warehousing
Secure, in-house storage and inventory management. Short and long-term warehousing solutions with full inventory visibility.

### Port Handling
Expert in-house port operations at Sri Lanka's key ports. Neptune's own port team manages every container movement.

### Roadways Cargo
Reliable inland transportation connecting ports, warehouses, and delivery points across Sri Lanka.
