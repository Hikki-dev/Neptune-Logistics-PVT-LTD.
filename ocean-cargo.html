FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions? Glad you asked

Clear answers regarding Port of Colombo ocean freight, container specifications, customs regulations, and booking procedures.

Full Container Load (FCL) reserves an entire dedicated container (20ft, 40ft, or 40HC) exclusively for your cargo, sealed from loading at your premises to discharge at the consignee’s facility. Less than Container Load (LCL) consolidates multiple smaller consignments into a shared container at our Colombo CFS warehouse, allowing you to pay only for the exact cubic meter (CBM) volume you consume.
Neptune Logistics is a certified, fully licensed Customs House Agent (CHA). Our in-house team directly processes ASYCUDA World entries, HS code classifications, duty assessments, and wharf gate passes with Sri Lanka Customs officers at the Port of Colombo. We never subcontract your customs declarations to external middlemen.
We have continuous operational clearance across all three container terminals at the Port of Colombo: Colombo International Container Terminals (CICT – deep-water facility capable of handling ultra-large container vessels), South Asia Gateway Terminals (SAGT), and Jaya Container Terminal (JCT).
Standard export requirements include the Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Export Customs Declaration (CusDec), Bill of Lading instructions, Verified Gross Mass (VGM) certificate, and any relevant statutory certifications (e.g. Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary inspection, or Tea Board clearance). Our documentation officers assist you with preparing all paperwork compliant with local and destination requirements.
Share your container number or Neptune reference with our Colombo operations desk by phone or WhatsApp, and our team will give you a direct status update covering factory gate-out, port terminal gate-in, customs release, vessel departure, and expected destination arrival.
