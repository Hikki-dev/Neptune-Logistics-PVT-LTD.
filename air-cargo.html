Questions? Glad you asked

Helpful answers on air cargo weight calculations, transit timelines, and airport customs clearance.

Aviation carriers charge based on either actual gross weight (kg) or volumetric weight (cubic dimensions divided by standard ratio 1:6000, or Length x Width x Height in cm divided by 6,000)—whichever is greater. Our operations team reviews your package dimensions in advance to advise you on the exact chargeable weight before dispatch.
Standard export cut-off at Katunayake BIA Air Cargo Village is typically 6 to 8 hours prior to scheduled flight departure. However, for urgent Express and AOG shipments, Neptune’s dedicated air customs team can facilitate emergency gate-in and priority screening up to 4 hours before departure.
Yes. Our air operations team is trained in international Dangerous Goods Regulations (DGR). We ensure complete compliance regarding UN packaging, dangerous goods declaration (DGD) forms, hazard labeling, and airline acceptance criteria.
