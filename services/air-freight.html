Frequently Asked Questions

What are the standard transit times for air freight?
Standard transit times range from 1 to 3 days for express priority service, and 4 to 6 days for economy/deferred routing, depending on the destination and flight availability.
Do you handle perishable and temperature sensitive goods?
Yes. We have specialized procedures for cool chain logistics, ensuring fresh fruits, seafood, cut flowers, and temperature sensitive pharmaceuticals are kept under strict climate control from origin to destination.
What is an AOG shipment?
AOG stands for "Aircraft on Ground". It is a high priority logistics classification for aircraft parts required immediately to resolve a grounded aircraft, managed under active 24/7 monitoring.
Do you offer full aircraft chartering?
Yes, for oversized cargo or critical supply runs, we can charter complete freighter aircraft or secure part charters through our extensive global aviation networks.
