Frequently Asked Questions

What types of cargo and transport capacities do you handle?
We handle all ranges of cargo, from small boxes to standard shipments originating from sea ports and air ports. Our logistics network utilizes a full range of transport vehicles, including prime movers, container trailers, and closed box trucks, ensuring secure delivery of any cargo scale.
Which regions of Sri Lanka do you cover?
We offer island wide transport. We operate daily routes between Colombo's ports/airports and all export processing zones (such as Katunayake, Biyagama, and Seethawaka), as well as deliveries to the Northern, Eastern, and Southern provinces.
Do you handle refrigerated container transport?
Yes, our prime movers can be equipped with clip on gensets to support refrigerated containers (reefers), maintaining temperature control for perishables in transit on the road.
How do you monitor roadway shipments?
Our transport office maintains direct mobile communication with drivers and active dispatch tracking, enabling real time status updates and arrival notifications for our customers.
