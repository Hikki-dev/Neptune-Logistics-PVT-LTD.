Ground Distribution

Seamless ground distribution networks

An efficient international shipment is only as fast as its final inland delivery. Neptune Logistics operates its own inland container haulage and roadways cargo distribution, connecting Sri Lanka's principal ports and airports to key factories, warehouses, and industrial zones.

We cover every scale, from small cargo distribution to large-capacity container haulage for manufacturing supplies, garments, and commercial retail goods, including repositioning of empty units, factory loading runs, and direct terminal deliveries.
