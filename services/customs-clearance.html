Navigating complexity with complete compliance

Customs regulations and tariff structures in Sri Lanka demand absolute precision. As a fully licensed Customs House Agent (CHA), Neptune Logistics provides end-to-end customs brokerage that minimizes transit delays, avoids costly penalties, and ensures correct duty valuations.

We handle every aspect of import, export, and cross-trade clearance, including BOI (Board of Investment) approvals, TIEP (Temporary Import for Export Processing) schemes, bonded warehouse declarations, and multi-country consolidations.

The Neptune Standard

We execute customs declarations using direct digital links to Sri Lanka Customs (ASYCUDA system), maintaining full security and direct oversight over your declarations through our dedicated customs experts. That means a single point of accountability, no third-party delays, and no markup chain, just Neptune's own team, answerable to you at every step.
