Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Customs House Agent (CHA)?
A Customs House Agent (CHA) is an entity licensed by the government customs department to act as a broker and representative for importers and exporters, preparing and submitting declarations and facilitating cargo clearance.
What documents are required for import clearance in Sri Lanka?
Generally required documents include the Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading or Airway Bill, Customs Declaration (CUSDEC), Delivery Order from the shipping line, and any specific import permits or licenses.
Can you handle Board of Investment (BOI) project clearances?
Yes. We have specialized customs brokers who manage duty free and duty concession imports under BOI approvals, handling the verification and processing from start to finish.
How quickly can customs clearance be completed?
With correct documentation, both air and sea freight are cleared within 24 hours of arrival. As soon as the goods arrive at the port, our dedicated team handles clearing the cargo within the same day.
