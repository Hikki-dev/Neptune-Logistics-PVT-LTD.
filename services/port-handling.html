Terminal Coordination

Direct, on-site terminal coordination

Port operations require immediate, physical coordination. Neptune Logistics maintains a dedicated physical presence at the Port of Colombo, Sri Lanka's central maritime gateway, resolving terminal holds, coordinating gate passes, and securing rapid delivery orders from shipping lines.

We manage the entire ground process, including terminal handling charges (THC), container transport bookings, customs inspections at port yards, and coordinate directly with terminal operators (SLPA, JCT, SAGT, CICT, CWIT) to ensure container loading and discharge timelines are met.

The Neptune Standard

Our port operations team is on site at terminal gates daily. We do not depend on remote clearing agents, we execute terminal processes ourselves, allowing us to bypass typical industry communication bottlenecks. A single point of accountability, no third-party delays, and no markup chain. Just Neptune's own team, answerable to you at every step.
